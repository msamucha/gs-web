Golf was always social. Now the app is too.

Most of golf happens around the round. The group chat, the friendly bet, the photo from the seventeenth, the score someone still owes you. Golfsocial keeps all of it in one place, with the people you actually play with. No ads, no noise, no strangers.

Inside the app

Built for the round, and everything around it.

01

The feed

Your people, their rounds, their photos. The Wednesday flight, the home club regulars, the travel four-ball, all in one place. No strangers, no algorithm.

02

The scorecard

Front nine, back nine, done. Stroke, Stableford or match play. Pars become birdies become a trend you can actually read, round after round.

03

The leaderboard

Weekend flights, the club championship, the season-long bet that never quite gets settled. Live scores, team standings, a real reason to play the back nine harder.

Official handicap

In partnership with Dansk Golf Union.

Connect once with your DGU number. Golfsocial keeps your official handicap up to date after every approved round. Scores submit themselves, marker approvals come through the app. No spreadsheets, no double entry, just the round, played and counted.
