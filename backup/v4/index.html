§02 — The Bulletin

A round is rarely played alone.

So the app shouldn't act like it is. Golfsocial is the place where the people you play with — your weekend flight, your Wednesday group, your travel four-ball — already are. Free on iOS and Android. No ads. No tricks. Just the app you wish you'd had since you started playing.

§03 — Inside the App

Three rooms of the club.

01

The Feed

Your people, their rounds, their photos. The Wednesday flight, the home-club regulars, the travel four-ball — all in one room, no strangers.

02

The Scorecard

Front nine, back nine, finished. Stroke, Stableford, match play. Pars become birdies become a trend you can actually read.

03

The Leaderboard

Tournaments, your weekend flight, the year-long club championship. Live scores, team standings, a real reason to play the back nine harder.

§04 — In-app awards

The rounds worth
remembering.

Inside the app, certain rounds quietly earn a mark — a private record of the moments worth keeping. Three of them, picked out below. There are more, but you have to play for them.

The Sub-Eighty Club

The first time the card finishes in the seventies. Once you're in, you're in for life.

The Birdie Streak

Three birdies in a row on the same round. Brief, rare, mostly remembered out loud.

The Par Machine

Nine pars on an eighteen. Quiet precision. The card that didn't need fireworks.

§05 — Partners

In partnership with Dansk Golf Union.

Your handicap, synced after every approved round.

Connect once with your DGU number. Golfsocial pulls your last twenty rounds automatically and keeps the official handicap up to date as you play. Scores submit themselves. Marker approvals come through the app. No double-entry, no exporting, no spreadsheets — just the round, played and counted.
