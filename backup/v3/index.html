§05 — Fixtures & Features

What's inside the app.

01

Rounds, properly tracked.

Scorecards that finish themselves. Stroke, Stableford, match play. Full history that travels with you, course to course.

02

Your circle, in one place.

Friends, groups, threads. The Wednesday flight has its own room. So does the travel four-ball. So does your home club.

03

One round, one thread.

Every round opens its own conversation, where your group goes after the 18th — no more screenshots in three different chats.

04

Numbers that mean something.

Strokes gained, putting average, greens in regulation. A 30-round trend, not a single-round mood swing.

05

Invitations, not group chats.

Tee time on Sunday at 14:20 — three of you in, one tap to confirm. The fourth seat fills itself.

06

A feed that's actually golf.

Only your people. Their rounds, their photos, their conversations. No ads. No strangers. Nothing for sale.

§07 — Membership

Free to join. Always will be.

Golfsocial is free on iOS and Android. No ads. No data resale. No upsell. The members are the product to no one.
