Live scoring. Follow the match as it happens.

~2 min read

Soon the whole group can follow the round in real time. Scores update hole by hole, the leaderboard moves while you play, and nobody has to ask what it stands at on the 14th tee. Live scoring is on its way to Golfsocial, free like the rest of it, and built so the match stays alive right to the last putt.

What live scoring will do

Once the round is underway, everyone sees the same leaderboard, updated the moment a score goes in. No waiting until the clubhouse, no doing the maths on a napkin, no doubt about who is actually leading. Whether you are in the group or following from the sofa, the match is right there, live.

Net scoring, so it stays close

A match is only fun when everyone has a shot at it. Live scoring works in net scoring, so players of different handicaps compete on level terms, the Stableford points or the net total doing the heavy lifting. The 20-handicapper can take down the scratch player, and the leaderboard stays interesting all the way home.

Why it will be better here

Live scoring without a subscription, sitting next to the official handicap and the social layer you already use. The round will not just be followed, it will be shared, argued over and remembered, then saved in your group for the rematch. This is one more step toward a single home for your golf.

When it lands

Live scoring is one of the next features arriving in Golfsocial. We are not putting a date on it, because we would rather ship it right than ship it loud. The app itself is already here, so the best move is simple: download Golfsocial now, and live scoring will be waiting the moment it goes live.

What to expect

  • Live leaderboard: everyone on the same screen, in real time.
  • Net scoring built in: different handicaps, level match.
  • Follow from anywhere: in the group or from the sofa.
  • Part of the same home: tied to your handicap and your group.
  • Free when it lands: no premium wall in front of the tension.
The match deserves an audience while it is still being played.
